## The format

### Is the pool a rolling window?

No, it is a fixed list that changes when it is announced. See [Rules](/rules).

### Are deckbuilding rules the same as Standard?

Yes: 60-card minimum maindeck, up to 15 in the sideboard, four copies of any card
except basic lands.

### Can I play any printing of a card?

Yes. If a card is legal, every printing of it is legal, including promos and
Secret Lair.

### Does Foundations rotate?

No. It is the permanent core set.

### What is banned?

See [Rules & legality](/rules).

## Playing

### Where are events?

Official events run on melee.gg or Challonge and are announced in the Discord. See
[Events](/events).

### The checker could not find a card in my list. Was it rejected?

No. The list is kept and that line is flagged until the name is fixed.

## The numbers

### Why does a win rate say "insufficient data"?

Too few games to mean anything. Every rate shows its sample size, and small
samples are hidden. See [Methodology](/methodology).

### What does "win rate of decks including this card" mean?

Exactly that. It describes the decks, not the card.

### Why doesn't an event affect the leaderboard?

Only Monthly events are rated, and only when they report pairings. See
[How ratings work](/ratings-explained).

### Can a rating be corrected by hand?

No. Ratings are recalculated from match results whenever anything changes.

## Contributing

### Can I write for the site?

Yes. Sign in and use **Write a community post** on the [Community](/community)
page. A new member's first posts are approved before they go live.

### I found a mistake in the data.

Report it in the Discord.
