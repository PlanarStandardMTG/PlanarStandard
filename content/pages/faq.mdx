## What is Planar Standard?

Planar Standard is a community-driven 60-card format which only includes cards
from the last two years of Universes Within sets, plus any evergreen sets
(currently only Foundations). Check [Rules & legality](/rules) to see the exact
sets and bans, if any.

## How do I find other players to play with?

Use any of the platform-specific LFG tags in find-a-match on the
[Discord](https://discord.gg/eeYH9XMCjT) to notify other players that you're
looking for a game. And hop on over to Channels & Roles to pick any of the roles
for yourself to be pinged when someone is looking for a game.

## What kind of tournaments are there?

We offer several types of tournaments. Note that all are being played on Arena:

- **Weekly Tournaments:** Casual tournaments where people can experiment with
  new strategies. Usually consisting of 3 to 4 rounds, or about 2 to 2.5 hours.
  We typically organise 2-3 Weeklies per week.
- **Monthly Championship Series:** Competitive tournaments with prize money for
  the first place, usually held on the first Saturday of each month. Consists of
  5 rounds of Swiss, followed by a cut for Top 8. The 5 rounds typically take
  3-4 hours, sometimes longer.
- **Planar Leagues:** Competitive long-term tournaments where you play around
  one game per week over the course of around 2 months.
- **Mid-month Madness:** Once-a-month tournaments with different deck building
  restrictions each month.
- **Legality Fracture:** Once-a-month tournaments where you can choose to play
  either a deck only of Universes Beyond sets (plus Foundations), or Planar
  Standard sets.

Make sure to check the tournament schedule in announcements on the Discord, on
[Reddit](https://www.reddit.com/r/planarMTG/) or on our [events page](/events)
to find the most recently announced dates and times.

## How do I join tournaments?

For the Weeklies we use [Challonge](https://challonge.com/), and share a link
you can register through for each event. You can find all the events on our
[events page](/events), under the events tab on the Discord, or on our community
page directly on Challonge. You need to have an account on Challonge to register
for the events.

For the Monthlies and custom tournaments we use [Melee](https://melee.gg/), where
you again need an account to take part in the events.

Planar Leagues use custom registration forms which will be announced in the
announcements channel a good while before they start.

**Important:** Melee will ask you your full name when registering on their site,
which, while you can hide from public view, will still be visible to Tournament
Organizers. Just something to keep in mind in case that is an issue for you.

## How can I support the community and help it grow?

Beyond engaging, learning, and playing (which helps more than you think!), you
can also donate to us through [Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/cw/PlanarMTG) or
[Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/planarmtg), which helps fund the website and
tournament prize pools. Or even just give our server a boost through your
Discord Nitro subscription. All supporters also get a nice name colour change in
the Discord server to show other members that you're one of the awesome people
helping us with all of this!

You can also help us out by heading on over to our official
[Twitch channel](https://www.twitch.tv/planarstandardmtg), following and
subscribing, and just hanging out with us. All proceeds from the channel also
support the Planar Standard tournament prize pools.

## Who is part of the Planar Standard team?

- **Mods:** They moderate the channel and save us from spambots.
- **Developers:** They develop our website and all of the related tools.
- **Planar Crew:** They organize and plan format-related initiatives.
- **Tournament Organizers:** They handle our events and make sure everyone has
  a good time.

## I also see some other roles on the Discord, could you elaborate?

- **Supporters:** Members who support our initiatives on Patreon and Ko-fi with
  $2 a month. They get to vote to decide the upcoming Mid-month Madness events.
- **Enthusiasts:** Members who support our initiatives on Patreon and Ko-fi with
  $5 a month. They get to vote to decide the upcoming Mid-month Madness events.
- **Walkers:** Members who support our initiatives on Patreon and Ko-fi with $10
  a month. They get to vote to decide the upcoming Mid-month Madness events and
  can submit decks closer to events.
- **Server Booster:** Members who help boost the server with Discord Boosts.
  They get to vote to decide the upcoming Mid-month Madness events.
- **Planar Twitch:** Gets pinged anytime a Planar streamer goes live. You can
  acquire it in Channels & Roles.
- **20 Questions:** Gets pinged anytime a game of 20 Questions starts. You can
  acquire it in Channels & Roles.
