This site is run by volunteers for the Planar Standard community. It is not a
business, it sells nothing, and it has no advertising and no analytics. What
follows is everything it knows about you and what you can do about it.

It is written to satisfy the UK GDPR and the EU GDPR. If something here is wrong
or unclear, that is a bug — please
[open an issue](https://github.com/planarstandard) or say so in
[the Discord](https://discord.gg/eeYH9XMCjT).

## You do not need an account

Every page of results, every rating, every article and every card is readable
without signing in, and reading is not logged against you. An account exists so
that you can **write** — publish an article, run an event, submit a deck. If you
never do those things you never have to give us anything.

## What we hold, if you do have an account

| What                                    | Why                                                          |
| --------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Your email address                      | to sign you in, and to email you a link when you ask for one |
| A hash of your password, if you set one | to check your password without storing it                    |
| Your provider account, if you used one  | so Google or Discord can vouch for you                       |
| Your display name, handle, bio, picture | your byline on anything you write                            |
| What you write and submit               | it is the site                                               |
| Sign-in times and IP addresses          | kept by our authentication provider, to stop abuse           |

**We never see your password.** It is checked against a one-way hash, which
cannot be turned back into what you typed. If you sign in with an emailed link
or with Google or Discord, there is no password at all.

Your display name, handle and bio are **public** — they appear next to anything
you publish, which is the point of them. Your email address is never shown on the
site.

### Tournament results are different

Results are recorded against the **handle you played under** on the organiser's
platform, not against your account here. They arrive already published from
Challonge or melee.gg. That is a deliberate design decision — it means the
competitive record does not depend on anybody having an account, and it means an
account can be deleted without the record of a tournament falling apart.

If you want a handle separated from your account, or a result corrected, ask.

## Why we are allowed to hold it

For your account and the things you publish, the lawful basis is **performance of
a contract**: you asked for an account so that you could use the site, and we
cannot give you one without holding an email address to sign you in.

For sign-in records and rate limiting, the basis is **legitimate interests** —
specifically, keeping the site from being abused. That is a narrow interest and
the data is a narrow set.

We do not rely on consent for anything, because we do not do anything that would
need it. There is nothing to opt out of.

## Cookies

One cookie, set only when you sign in, holding your session. It is marked
`HttpOnly`, so scripts cannot read it, and `Secure`, so it is never sent over an
unencrypted connection.

That cookie is **strictly necessary** — it is what signing in means — so there is
no consent banner, because there is nothing to consent to. There are no
analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking cookies on this site.

## Who else sees it

- **Supabase** stores the database and runs authentication.
- **Vercel** serves the site.
- **An email provider** delivers your sign-in and password-reset messages.

Each of these processes data on our instructions and for no purpose of their own.
Nothing is sold, and nothing is shared with anybody else.

Some of them run servers outside the UK and EU. Where that happens the transfer
relies on the provider's standard contractual clauses.

## How long we keep it

Your account and what you write stay until you delete them. Sign-in records are
kept by our authentication provider on its own retention schedule, which is
measured in weeks, not years.

Published articles and tournament results are kept indefinitely — they are the
community's record of what happened.

## What you can do

Signed in, from **[your data](/account/data)**:

- **See it and take a copy.** One JSON file with your profile, your articles
  including drafts nobody else can see, and your decks. That is the right of
  access and the right to portability in one button.
- **Correct it.** Your name, handle and bio are editable from
  [your profile](/profile) at any time.
- **Delete it.** This removes your email address, your password, every connected
  provider, and your name, handle, bio and picture. It cannot be undone.

You also have the right to object to processing, to ask us to restrict it, and to
complain to a regulator — in the UK the
[ICO](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/), or your own supervisory authority in
the EU. We would rather you told us first.

### What deleting does not remove

Anything you published **stays up, with your name replaced** by "Deleted member".
An article the community is still reading is not withdrawn because its author
left, and the audit trail behind a rating correction is not ours to erase either.
Once your name is off it, what remains identifies nobody.

Tournament results stay for the same reason, and because they were published by
the organiser rather than by us.

If you think something specific should go, ask — this is a volunteer project, not
a wall.

## Children

The site is not aimed at children and we do not knowingly hold data about anyone
under 13. If you think we do, tell us and it will be removed.

## Changes
