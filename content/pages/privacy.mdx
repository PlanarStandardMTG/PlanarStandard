This site is run by volunteers. It sells nothing and has no ads or analytics. It
is written to meet the UK and EU GDPR. Questions or corrections:
[GitHub](https://github.com/planarstandard) or
[the Discord](https://discord.gg/eeYH9XMCjT).

## You do not need an account

Everything is readable without signing in, and reading is not logged. An account
is only needed to write posts or save decks.

## What we hold if you have an account

| What                                    | Why                                     |
| --------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| Email address                           | to sign you in                          |
| A hash of your password, if you set one | to check your password                  |
| Your Google or Discord account, if used | to sign you in                          |
| Display name, handle, bio, picture      | your public byline                      |
| What you write and submit               | it is the site                          |
| Sign-in times and IP addresses          | kept by our auth provider to stop abuse |

We never see your password. Your name, handle and bio are public; your email is
never shown.

### Tournament results

Results are recorded against the handle you played under on melee.gg or
Challonge, not your account. They were already public there. Ask if you want a
handle unlinked or a result corrected.

## Lawful basis

Your account and posts: **performance of a contract**. Sign-in records and rate
limiting: **legitimate interests** in preventing abuse. We do not rely on consent.

## Cookies

One strictly necessary session cookie, set when you sign in (`HttpOnly`,
`Secure`). No tracking cookies, so no consent banner.

## Who else sees it

- **Supabase**: database and sign-in.
- **Vercel**: hosting.
- **An email provider**: sign-in and reset emails.

They process data only on our instructions. Some run servers outside the UK and
EU, covered by standard contractual clauses. Nothing is sold or shared otherwise.

## How long we keep it

Your account and posts stay until you delete them. Sign-in records are kept for
weeks. Published posts and tournament results are kept indefinitely.

## Your rights

From **[your data](/account/data)** you can:

- **Download** your profile, posts (including drafts) and decks as JSON.
- **Correct** your details on [your profile](/dashboard/profile).
- **Delete** your account: email, password, connected providers, name, handle,
  bio and picture. This cannot be undone.

You can also object to or restrict processing, or complain to the
[ICO](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/) or your EU supervisory authority.

### What deletion keeps

Published posts stay up under "Deleted member". Tournament results and rating
correction records also stay. Ask if you think something specific should go.

## Children

The site is not for children under 13. Tell us if we hold such data and we will
remove it.

## Changes

This page changes through public pull requests. Material changes are announced
in the Discord.
