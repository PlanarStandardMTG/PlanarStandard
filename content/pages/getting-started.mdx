## 1. Check the pool

Six sets: Foundations plus five that rotate. [Rules & legality](/rules) has the
list. If a card is legal, any printing of it is fine.

## 2. Build a deck

[Browse decks](/decks) others have played, or build around a card you like.

## 3. Find a game

Official events are announced in the Discord and listed on [Events](/events).
Casual games happen in the Discord all the time.

## 4. Save your list

[Import your deck](/decks?view=mine) to check it against the pool. Unknown card
names are flagged, not rejected.

## 5. Write something

Tournament reports, primers and card takes are welcome on
[Community](/community).

## Links

- **Discord**: events, games and questions.
- **[r/planarMTG](https://www.reddit.com/r/planarMTG/)**: announcements and
