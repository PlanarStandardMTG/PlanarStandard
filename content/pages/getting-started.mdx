## 1. Read the pool

Six sets, Foundations plus five rotating ones. [Rules & legality](/rules) has the
list and the construction rules — 60-card maindeck, 15-card sideboard, four
copies of anything but basics.

The one rule worth internalising early: **legality is about the card, not the
printing**. If a card is in a legal set, the copy you already own is fine
whatever set it came from.

## 2. Build something

The pool is small enough that you can read all of it, which is unusual and worth
taking advantage of. Two starting points:

- **Copy a deck.** The [metagame pages](/community) show what people are actually
  bringing to events, with the decklists attached.
- **Build from a card you like.** In a six-set pool, one strong card usually has
  a deck around it that nobody has found yet. This works far more often here than
  it does in a larger format.

Budget is rarely the blocker. Because any printing is legal, the expensive part of
most constructed formats — chasing a specific printing — does not apply.

## 3. Find a game

Events are run by community organisers and announced in the Discord, weekly and
monthly. Most are on melee; some on Challonge. Both report full pairings, which
is what lets a result count toward [ratings](/ratings-explained).

Casual games happen constantly in the Discord between events. You do not need a
finished deck to ask for one.

## 4. Submit your list

Submitting a decklist takes a minute and is what makes the metagame pages exist.
An unsubmitted list is a game that happened and a data point that did not.

Lists are checked against the pool when they arrive. If a card name does not
resolve, the list is kept and flagged rather than rejected — you will be asked
about that one line, not made to retype the deck.

## 5. Write something

The [Community](/community) feed is open to the community: tournament reports,
deck primers, a case that everyone is wrong about a card. You do not need to be
an established name and you do not need to be right. Ask in the Discord and
someone will get you set up.

## Where to go

- **Discord** — events, pickup games, deckbuilding, and the fastest answer to any
  question here.
- **[r/planarMTG](https://www.reddit.com/r/planarMTG/)** — announcements and
  longer discussion.
- **[Resources](/resources)** — the tools worth having open while you build.
