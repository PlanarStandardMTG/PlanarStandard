## 1. Check the pool

Two years of Universes Within sets plus FDN. [Rules & legality](/rules)
has the list. If a card is legal, any printing of it is fine.

## 2. Build a deck

[Browse decks](/decks) others have played, or brew your own.

## 3. Find a game

Official events are announced in the Discord and listed on [Events](/events).

## 4. Save your list

[Import your deck](/decks?view=mine) to confirm legality.

## 5. Help shape the community

Tournament reports, primers and card takes are welcome on
[Community](/community).
