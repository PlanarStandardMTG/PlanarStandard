## Community

- **Discord** — events, pickup games, deckbuilding help, and where announcements
  land first.
- **[r/planarMTG](https://www.reddit.com/r/planarMTG/)** — announcements and
  longer-form discussion.
- **[GitHub](https://github.com/planarstandard)** — this site, the format data,
  and every statistic's source.

## Building a deck

- **[Browse the legal pool on Scryfall](https://scryfall.com/search?q=game%3Apaper+%28set%3AFDN+or+set%3ADFT+or+set%3ATDM+or+set%3AEOE+or+set%3AECL+or+set%3ASOS%29&unique=cards&as=grid&order=color)** —
  every card currently legal, as a grid. The query is the six-set pool; add your
  own filters onto it.
- **[Scryfall syntax reference](https://scryfall.com/docs/syntax)** — worth ten
  minutes if you have never used it. `f:pauper`-style format filters do not apply
  here, so search by set.
- **[Rules & legality](/rules)** — the pool, the construction rules, and how
  printings work.

## Reading the numbers

- **[Methodology](/methodology)** — every metric definition, published verbatim
  from the code's own documentation.
- **[How ratings work](/ratings-explained)** — the Elo system, what counts as a
  rated match, and why ratings are never adjusted by hand.

## Running events

- **[Running an event](/organizers)** — including the melee 60-day export
  warning, which is the one thing on this site worth reading before you need it.
- **[melee.gg](https://melee.gg/)** — where most events run.
- **[Challonge](https://challonge.com/)** — brackets for smaller events.
