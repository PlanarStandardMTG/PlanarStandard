Planar Standard is a constructed Magic format with a smaller card pool than
Standard and a faster rotation. Six sets are legal at a time, with
**Foundations** as a permanent core. See [Rules & legality](/rules).

A smaller pool makes decks cheaper to build, and each new set actually moves the
metagame.

## This site

- **Results and decklists** from official events.
- **An Elo leaderboard** built from match results.
- **A decklist checker** for the legal pool.
- **[News](/news)** and **[community posts](/community)**.

Every rate is shown with its sample size, and rates with too few games are
hidden. See [Methodology](/methodology).

## Who runs it

The format is organised through the community Discord and
[r/planarMTG](https://www.reddit.com/r/planarMTG/). Official events are run by
the organising group's tournament organisers. Any member can
[write a community post](/community).
