Planar Standard is a community Magic: the Gathering format built around a smaller card
pool with a faster rotation. Two years of Universes Within sets plus
**Foundations** as a permanent core. See [Rules & legality](/rules).

## This site

- **Results and decklists** from events.
- **An Elo leaderboard** built from rated tournaments.
- **A decklist checker/importer** for the format.
- **[News](/news)** and **[community posts](/community)**.

## Who runs it

The format is organised through the community [Discord](https://discord.gg/eeYH9XMCjT) and
[r/planarMTG](https://www.reddit.com/r/planarMTG/). Official events are run by
the organising group's tournament organisers. Any member can
[write a community post](/community).
