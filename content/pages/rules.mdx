Planar Standard plays by the ordinary rules of constructed Magic. What differs is
the card pool and the rotation schedule.

## The card pool

Six sets are legal:

| Code | Role |
|---|---|
| `FDN` | Foundations — the permanent core set |
| `DFT` | rotating |
| `TDM` | rotating |
| `EOE` | rotating |
| `ECL` | rotating |
| `SOS` | rotating |

This is a **defined list**, not a rolling window computed from release dates. A
special event may declare its own pool — a single-set gauntlet, a throwback to an
older season — without that changing the format.

## Deckbuilding

- **Maindeck:** 60 cards minimum, no maximum.
- **Sideboard:** 15 cards maximum.
- **Copies:** at most 4 of any card across maindeck and sideboard, counted by
  name. Basic lands are exempt.

## Legality is about the card, not the printing

A card is legal if **any printing of it appears in a legal set**. Once it is
legal, **any printing may be played** — promo, Secret Lair, an old core set,
whatever is in your binder.

So `Llanowar Elves` is legal because it is in Foundations, and the copy you play
can be the M19 one. A decklist that writes `Llanowar Elves (M19)` is accepted:
the set code on the line is recorded as provenance and never has to resolve.

This keeps the format cheap to enter, which is most of the point of a small pool.
Un-set cards and Secret Lair printings are covered by the same rule — the oracle
card is what is legal, and its printings are all equivalent.

## Bans

Bans are **data, not code**: a Banned & Restricted announcement changes rows that
an organiser edits, and never requires a site deploy. Bans, pool changes, and
one-off legal-set exceptions all work this way.

The live banlist will be published on this page directly from those rows, so it
cannot go stale after an announcement. Until that is wired up, current bans are
announced in the community Discord and on
[r/planarMTG](https://www.reddit.com/r/planarMTG/).

## Rotation

Planar Standard is intended to always hold the previous two years of Universes
Within sets. Rotation coincides with the release of the first Universes Within
set of each year.

**Foundations does not rotate.** It is the format's core set, and it is what keeps
the pool from losing its basic effects every year.

## Checking a list

A list can be checked against the pool before an event rather than during it. The
validator reads card names, resolves them against the legal pool, and reports
what it could not place — a misspelling, a card outside the pool, or a copy count
over the limit. Anything it cannot resolve is reported rather than silently
dropped.
