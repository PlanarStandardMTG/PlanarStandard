## Deckbuilding

- **Maindeck:** 60 cards minimum.
- **Sideboard:** up to 15 cards.
- **Copies:** up to 4 of any card by name, across maindeck and sideboard. Basic
  lands are exempt.

## Any printing is legal

A card is legal if any printing of it is in a legal set, and then any printing of
it may be played. `Llanowar Elves (M19)` is legal because Llanowar Elves is in
Foundations.

## Rotation

The format holds the previous two years of Universes Within sets.
Rotation happens when the first Universes Within set of each year releases.

The format also holds the latest "Core" set.

## Checking a list

[Paste a list](/decks/new) to check it; you don't need an account. Misspellings,
cards outside the pool and too many copies are all detectable.
